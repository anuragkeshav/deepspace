How we look

The universe does not reveal itself all at once. We observe the light, trace its journey, and reconstruct distant worlds from what reaches us.

ASTRA / DEEP FIELD

The farther we look, the older the light becomes.

Every distant observation is also a glimpse into the past — a record of something that happened long before its light reached us.

Distance

Looking farther into space means looking farther back in time. The light reaching our instruments may have travelled for millions or billions of years.

Light

Light is information. Its brightness, colour, movement and spectrum can reveal temperature, composition, velocity and enormous cosmic structures.

Time

Astronomy is also archaeology. The farther away an object is, the further back into cosmic history its observed light can take us.
